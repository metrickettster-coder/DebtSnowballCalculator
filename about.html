About This Calculator

Why it exists, how it works, and what it isn't.

What this is

A free tool for planning how to pay off multiple debts. Enter your balances, interest rates, and minimum payments, and it shows you a month-by-month plan comparing the debt snowball method (smallest balance first), the debt avalanche method (highest interest rate first), and anything in between.

Why it exists

Most debt payoff calculators make you pick one strategy up front and commit to it, or only handle one debt at a time. This one runs the full simulation for every strategy at once so you can see the actual trade-off — in debt-free date and total interest — for your specific numbers, instead of relying on generic advice about which method is "better."

How it works

Everything runs as plain JavaScript in your browser. There's no backend, no account, and no database — the calculation you see is happening on your device, not on a server somewhere. The full source is visible in your browser's developer tools, and the project's source repository is public (see Contact). See the privacy policy for exactly what is and isn't stored.

What this isn't

This is a calculator, not financial advice. It projects outcomes based on the numbers you enter and the assumptions built into the simulation (fixed rates, consistent payments); it doesn't know your full financial situation and can't account for rate changes, fees, or life events. See the Terms page for the full disclaimer.

Who maintains it

This is an independently built, free tool — not a bank, lender, or financial institution, and not affiliated with one. It doesn't sell financial products, and as of this writing it doesn't run ads. If that ever changes, the privacy policy will be updated to say so before it happens.
