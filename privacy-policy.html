Privacy Policy

Plain language, no legal padding. This page describes exactly what happens to your data on this site today, and will be updated if that ever changes.

Last updated: September 2026.

The short version

This calculator does its math entirely in your browser. Your debts, balances, and interest rates are never sent to a server — we don't have one to send them to. The one exception is the contact form on the Contact page: if you choose to use it, what you type there is sent to a third-party form service, described below.

What's stored, and where

  • Browser local storage. The debts and settings you enter are saved to your own browser's local storage so they're still there next time you visit this site on the same device and browser. This data never leaves your device and isn't accessible to us.
  • Share links. If you use the "Share plan" button, your debt details are encoded directly into the URL you copy. Anyone you send that link to can see the numbers in it — treat a share link the same way you'd treat a screenshot of your finances.
  • No accounts, no server-side storage. There's no sign-up, no login, and no database. We have no record of who has used this calculator or what numbers they entered.

Third-party scripts

This site loads one third-party script: Chart.js, served from the jsDelivr CDN, used to draw the payoff timeline chart. Like any request to a third-party server, loading this script exposes standard technical information to jsDelivr (such as your IP address and browser type) the same way any website request does. It does not set cookies or track you on this site.

Contact form

The form on the Contact page is handled by Formspree, a third-party form service — this site has no server of its own to receive it. If you submit that form, whatever you enter (name, email, and message) is sent directly to Formspree and then to whoever maintains this site; it is not stored anywhere else by this site. This only happens if you choose to fill out and submit that form — it has no effect on using the calculator itself. See Formspree's own site for their privacy policy and how they handle submitted data.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies. If that changes — for example, if we add advertising or analytics in the future — this policy will be updated first, and a consent notice will be added for visitors where required by law before any such change goes live.

Advertising

This site does not currently run any advertising. If that changes, this section will describe what ad providers are used, what data they collect (typically including cookies or device identifiers for ad personalization), and how to opt out or manage ad settings, before any ads go live.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store any personal data on our servers, there's nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on request — there's no server-side record to act on. Anything saved locally (browser storage, a share link you sent) is entirely within your own control: clearing your browser data removes it, and simply not sharing a link keeps its contents private.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or loads ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect it before the change takes effect, and the "last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.

Contact

This is an independent, free tool. For questions about this policy, use the contact method listed wherever you found this site.

Your numbers stay on your device — this calculator does not send data to any server.
