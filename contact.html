This is a free, independently maintained tool, not a company with a support team — there's no guaranteed response time, and no phone or live chat support exists. Issues and requests are read, but not every one will get a reply or be acted on.

What this isn't for

This isn't a channel for personal financial advice — the people maintaining this tool aren't your financial advisor and can't advise on your specific situation. For that, see a licensed financial professional. See Terms for the full disclaimer.

For anything about how your data is handled, see the privacy policy first — most questions along those lines are already answered there.

Your numbers stay on your device — this calculator does not send data to any server.
