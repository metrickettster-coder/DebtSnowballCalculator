Free tools we trust

Useful, free resources for getting out of debt. We're not paid by or affiliated with any of them.

  • AnnualCreditReport.com

    The official, federally authorized site for your free credit reports from all three bureaus. Check for errors that could be hurting your score.

  • Credit Karma

    Free credit scores and alerts, so you can watch your score recover as you pay debts down.

  • National Foundation for Credit Counseling (NFCC)

    Find a nonprofit credit counselor for a free budget review and, if it fits, a debt management plan that can lower card interest.

  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)

    Plain-language government guides on credit cards, debt collection and consolidation, plus a place to file complaints.

  • Federal Student Aid repayment plans

    Official info on federal student loan repayment options before you add them to a payoff plan.
